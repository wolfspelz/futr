# Why "Polity"

We considered renaming the concept currently called "Polity" because the term leans political rather than ethnic. A polity is a named entity (people, empire, organization) that persists across time while its political institutions change. Civilizations are snapshots of a polity at a specific point in time.

## The Problem

No single English word cleanly covers both "a people" (Andorians) and "a multi-people political union" (Federation of Planets).

## Alternatives Considered

- **People** — Natural for ethnic groups (Andorians, Germans, Vulcans) but breaks down for multi-ethnic unions like the Federation. "The Federation people" sounds wrong.
- **Nation** — Precise academic distinction from "state," but feels Earth-centric for alien species and doesn't fit non-human or non-political entities.
- **Faction** — Implies being part of something larger, not a standalone entity.
- **Group** — Too vague.
- **Entity** — Maximally broad and correct, but bland and clinical.
- **Actor** — From international relations theory. Covers states, peoples, organizations, but unfamiliar.
- **Society** / **Culture** — Too narrow. A Matrioshka brain isn't a "culture."
- **Realm** / **Domain** / **Power** — Imply territory or force, not identity.
- **Collective** — Borg connotations in the Star Trek context.
- **Civilization** — Already taken for the time-snapshot concept.

## Decision

Stick with **Polity**. The political connotation is arguably accurate: even ethnic entries like "Andorians" are tracked as organized political actors (government, territory, fleet, population). An Andorian living outside the Empire wouldn't be part of the entry. What we model is political units, not biology or culture.
