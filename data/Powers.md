# Why "Power"

The persistent entity between a universe and its civilizations is called a **Power**. A power is a named entity (people, empire, organization, planet, even a single individual) that persists across time. Civilizations are snapshots of a power at a specific point in time: "Federation 2373" is the Federation of Planets in the year 2373.

## The Problem

No single English word cleanly covers both "a people" (Andorians) and "a multi-people political union" (Federation of Planets), while also fitting a lone archailect or a planet.

The concept was called "Polity" until 2026-10. Polity reads as "a political organization", which is one aspect of an entity rather than its container, and in everyday use it sounds smaller than a civilization, not larger.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Polity** — Size-agnostic in political science, but reads as "a political unit" and sounds smaller than civilization.
- **People** — Natural for ethnic groups (Andorians, Vulcans) but breaks down for multi-species unions like the Federation, and drags in peoples/races debates.
- **Nation** — Earth-centric, same ethnic problem as people.
- **Faction** — Implies being part of something larger, not a standalone entity.
- **Society** — Neutral, but sounds smaller than civilization and has no time signal.
- **Realm** / **Domain** — Imply territory; awkward for an individual or a nomadic fleet.
- **Actor** — Precise (international relations term), but dry and unfamiliar.
- **Lineage** — Captures the time dimension, but sounds biological.
- **Culture** / **Sphere** / **Dominion** / **Collective** — Collide with names in our own data (The Culture, Terragen Sphere, the Dominion, the Borg Collective).
- **Civilization** — Already taken for the time-snapshot concept.

## Decision

**Power**, as in "the great powers". It is short, neutral, scales from an individual to a galactic empire, and implies neither ethnicity nor a particular form of government. Its weak point is that "power" is also a physical quantity used in Kardashev calculations, so in texts about energy write "power output" or "watts" and reserve the bare word for the entity.
