FUTR

Fictional Universe Taxonomy Research

A galactic archive cataloging the civilizations, empires, and peoples of science fiction’s greatest universes.

Our Mission
The FUTR project collects statistics about civilizations in science fiction universes. We catalog population counts, territorial reach, technological levels, and more — building a comprehensive database of the fictional peoples that have shaped our imagination.
