The Star Kingdom of Manticore during the events of “The Honor of the Queen” (1903 PD), on the eve of war with the People’s Republic of Haven. Captain Honor Harrington commands HMS Fearless on a diplomatic mission to Grayson, leading to the Treaty of Alliance between Manticore and the Protectorate of Grayson. This period marks Manticore’s transition from peaceful trading power to wartime footing, as the expansionist People’s Republic of Haven threatens the Manticoran Wormhole Junction.

  2. Government

    Text Canon
    Constitutional MonarchyHouse of Winton · since 1485 PD

    The Star Kingdom operates as a constitutional monarchy established in 1485 PD when Roger Winton was crowned King Roger I.

    Full entry · 2 references

    The government consists of:

    • The Monarch (House of Winton) as head of state with Royal Prerogative powers;
    • A bicameral Parliament with House of Lords (upper) and House of Commons (lower);
    • A Prime Minister leading the government via parliamentary majority;
    • The Royal Council (Cabinet) advising the Crown.

    The Constitution requires heirs to the throne to marry commoners to maintain connection with the people.

  3. Kardashev

    K scale Calculated guess
    1.1Type I · range 1.0 – 1.2

    Estimated based on economic output relative to Sol System in 1900 PD: Manticore’s GSP was 78% of Sol’s GSP (the highest in human space).

    Full entry · 2 references

    Manticore achieved this with ~3.2 billion people vs Sol’s 10+ billion. Per-capita economic output approximately 2–3x Sol’s. Technology includes interstellar travel, fusion power, and advanced industry. Sol System in 1900 PD is an advanced interstellar hub, estimated at K~1.1–1.3.

    Manticore at 78% of that economic output with 1/3 the population indicates very high energy usage per capita. However, neither civilization harvests stellar-scale energy (not Type II), placing Manticore solidly in the Type I range at approximately K=1.1.

    • Honorverse Wiki – Manticore Wormhole Junction
    • Honorverse Wiki – Gross System Product

  8. Techlevel

    TL 1–15 Informed guess
    TL 9FTL · gravitics

    TL 9 FTL. Hyperspace FTL, impeller wedge drives (gravitics), grav-pulse communications.

    Full entry

    Hardest tech: impeller wedge, i.e. artificial gravity manipulation for propulsion and defense. No replicators, no transporters, no matter-energy conversion technology. Manticoran Wormhole Junction is a natural phenomenon, not built technology. Advanced missile tech (multi-drive missiles) but still kinetic/energy weapons.
