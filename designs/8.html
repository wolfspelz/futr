Notice to All Transit Applicants

All entities operating energy harvesting infrastructure exceeding K-Class 1.0 must complete Declaration Form MTA-E7 prior to transit authorization. Failure to declare planetary holdings in excess of 50 worlds constitutes a Class 4 Transit Violation under Multiverse Immigration Code §2.17.4.

Non-biological entities (robotic, AI, or uploaded consciousness) require supplementary Form MTA-S3 and must pass Sentience Verification Protocol before permits are stamped.
