Imperium of Man Spans One Million Worlds

Holy Terra's dominion dwarfs all recorded civilizations in sheer territorial reach; Ork incursions contest an estimated two million additional planets

At a Glance: Imperium M41

Planets1,000,000
GovernmentTheocratic Autocracy
KardashevII+
Threat LevelExterminatus-capable

The Imperium of Man, sovereign dominion of the God-Emperor of Mankind, now governs exactly one million worlds across the Milky Way galaxy — a figure that eclipses every other civilization in the FUTR index by an order of magnitude. The datum, confirmed by Administratum census tabulators, represents the largest single territorial holding ever recorded.

Yet Imperial authorities remain unable to secure their borders. The Ork menace, a decentralised network of Waaaghs numbering an estimated two million contested planets, continues to grow unchecked. Unlike the Imperium's rigid hierarchy, Ork forces operate without unified government or central command — a fact that makes their aggregate territorial footprint all the more alarming.

— From our correspondent at Segmentum Solar

Late Edition

Federation Holds Steady at 150 Member Worlds

The United Federation of Planets, reporting from stardate 50893.5 (year 2373), maintains 150 member worlds with a combined population estimated at ~100 billion. The figure, classified as an informed guess by FUTR analysts, places the Federation as the preeminent Alpha Quadrant power — though dwarfed by Gamma and Delta Quadrant empires.

Culture Population Exceeds 30 Trillion

The Culture (pre-Idiran War snapshot) reports a population of approximately 30 trillion citizens across an estimated 14,000 Banks Orbitals. The anarcho-communist hyper-civilisation remains the benchmark for post-scarcity governance in the FUTR index.

Terragen Census Defies Notation

The Terragen Sphere (Orion's Arm) has filed a population figure of 3.9×1017 — a number so vast it overwhelms standard demographic tables. FUTR actuaries note this figure encompasses biological, embodied, mechanical, and virtual populations combined.

Thirty Billion on Earth, Four Billion on Mars: A System Divided

The United Nations of Earth reports a population of 30 billion as of 2350, while the Martian Congressional Republic maintains 4 billion citizens on its terraforming world. The two inner planets remain locked in a cold war that shapes every aspect of Sol system politics.

Humanity's Long Arc: Ten Snapshots Across 300,000 Years

The FUTR index tracks ten snapshots of human civilisation — from the Early Pleistocene through the Stone Age, Bronze Age, Classic Age, Middle Ages, Age of Sail, Industrialization, the 20th Century, the 1980s, and the present day (2023). The arc from a few thousand individuals to 8 billion remains our baseline for measuring every fictional civilisation in the index.

The Culture (Pre-War)

The Culture's anarcho-AI civilisation, snapshotted before the Idiran War, hosts ~30 trillion citizens across ~14,000 Banks Orbitals. No formal government; ship Minds govern by consensus. Kardashev III.

Firefly: The Alliance 2517

The Anglo-Sino Alliance governs 215 worlds with a population of 303 billion. A single-system civilisation — all planets orbit the same star cluster.

Perry Rhodan: Two Empires

The Solares Imperium (human, 2040) coexists with the Großes Imperium — the Arkonide alien empire, population 50 trillion. The Posbis (2114), a cyborg civilisation, represent a third tracked entity.

Honorverse

Manticore 1903 PD and Haven 1903 PD — two interstellar powers locked in a generational war. Naval strength is the defining metric.

Orion's Arm: Beyond Scale

The Terragen Sphere (3.9×1017 pop) defies comparison. Sub-entries include the Solar System and the Diamond Network — a machine intelligence civilization. Dyson spheres and megastructures are commonplace.

Warhammer 40K: The Ork Question

Orks M41 contest ~2 million planets (calculated estimate). Decentralised Waaaghs, no unified government — each warband operates independently. Not an empire; a species-wide impulse.
