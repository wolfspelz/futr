About Federation 2373

The United Federation of Planets

The United Federation of Planets is an interstellar federal republic composed of planetary governments that agreed to exist semi-autonomously under a single central government based on the principles of universal liberty, rights, and equality, and to share their knowledge and resources in peaceful cooperation, scientific development, and mutual defense.

By 2373, the Federation had grown to include approximately 150 member worlds and thousands of colonies spread across 8,000 light-years. This was also the year the Dominion War began, a conflict that would test the Federation's ideals and military capability to their limits. The year represents a critical snapshot: a mature interstellar democracy at the peak of its expansion, about to face its greatest existential challenge.

Technologically, the Federation possesses warp drive capable of exceeding Warp 9, matter-energy conversion (replicators and transporters), and sophisticated artificial intelligence. Their tech level of 9 reflects mastery of fundamental forces, though they remain below civilizations that have achieved megastructure engineering or substrate-independent consciousness.
