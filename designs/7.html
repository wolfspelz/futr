Star Trek

Designation: ST • Civilizations monitored: 4 • Classification: TOP SECRET

Surveillance Imagery — ST

Source: Deep Space Relay 47 (redacted) • Capture date: Stardate 50893.5

Four civilizations monitored across two temporal snapshots (2063, 2161, 2373). All subjects are warp-capable. Dominant technologies include faster-than-light drive, directed energy weapons, and matter/energy conversion. Largest entity (Federation 2373) spans ~150 member worlds. Three subjects are founding members of the Federation alliance.

United Federation of Planets 2373

  • Population: ~1 × 1011 (est.)
  • Planets: ~150 member worlds
  • Warships: No confirmed figure (redacted)
  • Kardashev: ~1.0
  • Government: Federal Republic

Population estimate unreliable — range spans 3×1010 to 1×1012. Figures derived from member world extrapolation. Confidence: LOW. — Analyst Petrov (redacted)

Klingon Empire 2161

  • Population: ~9 × 109
  • Planets: ~24
  • Kardashev: ~0.9
  • Government: Imperial Monarchy

No primary source for 22nd-century census. Figure derived from homeworld data + colony extrapolation. Confidence: LOW.

Andorian Empire 2161

  • Population: ~8 × 109 (range 5B–11B)
  • Planets: 14 (homeworld + 13 colonies)
  • Kardashev: ~0.9
  • Techlevel: 9 (FTL) — Kumari-class cruisers, warp 5+
  • Government: Constitutional Monarchy

Federation founding member. Homeworld is icy moon Andoria (Procyon system). Known disputed territory with Vulcans (Weytahn). Population derived from non-canon sources. Confidence: LOW.

Vulcans 2063

  • Population: No data (redacted)
  • Planets: 1 (homeworld only)
  • Kardashev: No data (redacted)
  • Techlevel: 9 (FTL) — mature warp-capable civilization
  • Government: Vulcan High Command (dissolved ~2161) (redacted)

Data collection for this subject remains critically incomplete. Subject uncooperative with census protocols. Most metrics unconfirmed. — Analyst Chen (redacted)
