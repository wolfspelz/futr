About: Federation 2373

The United Federation of Planets

The United Federation of Planets is an interstellar federal republic composed of planetary governments that agreed to exist semi-autonomously under a single central government based on the principles of universal liberty, rights, and equality. It shares many qualities with its founding members, particularly the values of the United Earth government.

By the year 2373, the Federation had grown to include approximately 150 member worlds spread across 8,000 light-years of the Alpha and Beta Quadrants. Its population encompassed trillions of sentient beings from hundreds of species, united under a post-scarcity economy and governed from Paris, Earth.

The Federation's Starfleet serves as its primary exploratory, scientific, diplomatic, and defensive arm, operating from starbases and utilizing warp-capable vessels for faster-than-light travel. At Class 10, the Federation represents a mature interstellar civilization with significant technological capability but still limited to its local galactic region.
