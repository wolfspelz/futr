The FUTR Project

The FUTR project collects statistics about civilizations in science fiction universes. Population counts, Kardashev scale ratings, territory sizes, fleet strengths, and more — all cataloged, sourced, and compared.

From the Federation of Planets to the Imperium of Man. From Firefly's Alliance to the Terragen Sphere. How do they compare to each other — and to us?

Open source. Community driven. Every datapoint referenced. We track confidence levels from hard canon to wild guesses.
