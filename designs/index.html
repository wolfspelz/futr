#8 Shrine

A digital devotional space treating fictional civilizations with reverence. Deep warm void with candlelight amber glow and vermillion accents. All 30 civilization names float as a constellation — font-weight mapped to log(population), text-shadow glow mapped to Kardashev rating. Crimson Pro variable + Outfit typography, horizontal-scrolling universe altars, flame-bar comparative charts, smoke gradient transitions, threshold dividers, and "illuminate, offer, meditate" interaction language. Reality's civilizations form a hidden arc of human progress.
